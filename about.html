Who is Jewel Reese?

Currently, I’m a third-year engineering student at the University of Waterloo, contributing at Disco AI, a Series A startup backed by Golden Ventures and Inovia Capital that’s building an AI-first social learning platform for B2B SaaS.

Previously, I’ve worked on B2C products at Intuit (TurboTax)↗ and RBC, launching tax filing and investor tools used at scale, and held leadership roles at UW Women in Engineering Committee and UW Product Management Club.

In other times, you’ll usually find me golfing, exploring new cafés, or at a concert—and lately, expanding my product mindset through books like UnSelling.

Have any questions or just want to chat? Feel free to contact me, I would love to meet you!

I am currently exploring Winter 2026 internship opportunities:)
